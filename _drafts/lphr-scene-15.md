# Intro

If we were simply reading the book, we would already be done with
adventure 01. But we're playing, and there is a vital part of the adventure's
capstone dungeon delve that I've been putting off until now: the looting!

I've ran this delve as a "brutal raid", so our PCs were mainly interested in
clearing the cultists infesting the place as fast as possible, and whenever they
found some loot they just made a mental note to get to it later.

Well, Nox has been defeated, and it's "Later" now. The book doesn't really
mention how often the diabolists here checked back with Thrune, but I figure our
heroes have enough time to gather and tally all the loot, though I guess they
might need to call in a couple of Silver Raven teams to help haul it all back to
base.

# [GM & Player] Dungeon Fantasy: The Looting

Unfortunately, the very fact that Thrune's minions know where this place is and
have successfully raided it in the past means that they cannot use the actual
monastery as a base, so they have to stay in the somewhat cramped Wasp Nest for
now.

But the looting can still happen! Let's go through each of the dungeon's rooms
in reverse order, listing the available loot and describing what the PCs do with
it.

## Room F7: Meditation Garden

This is where we start, with the PCs just having killed Nox and finished taking
a breather. It looks like Nox was reading before the alarm was raised.

### [GM] XP Awards

- +1 for recovering the soul tomes (claimed)

- +1 for discovering the clue contained within (check later).


### [GM] The treasure

The books are soul tomes created by Yiliv the Scrivenite. They became mundane
books when the original owners of their memories died, but remain valuable for
the knowledge they contain.

We also have Nox's possessions, originally described [here][2].

So we get this list, with prices and weights formatted for the use of my
home-grown tallying scripts:

- Soul Tomes: +4 to History or Area Knowledge rolls about Kintargo and Ravounel
  if consulted. |$16000, 10kg|.

- Nox's Armor: DR 7 to the front of the body, 4/2 everywhere else except the
  head. |$5425, 13.5kg|.

- Belt pouch. |$10, 0.1kg|
  - 20 x gold coin. |$400, 0.01kg| (total $8000, 0.20kg)

- Dwarven Dueling Glaive: Damage sw+2 cut (Reach 1,2) or thr+3 imp (Reach
  1,2). |$400, 3kg|.

Nox actually used a different suit of armor during the fight with DR 6/4 all
over, as the stats I had for her were an earlier version. But let's use the
published one as loot.

### [Player] The Looting

Jania identifies the books as soul tomes, and notices that their titles are the
names of the people they were taken from. Two of them bear the sane surname as
Rexus, and Urist also recognizes a lot of the others. It's pretty safe to assume
that the people who were the source of the books are now dead, as they don't
give off a magic aura.

They strip Nox of her armor and weapons, with Jania insisting they take her coin
purse right away. The rest is heavy and in the case of the equipment too full of
Asmodean symbols for them to want to use it right away, so they keep it in a
neat pile and tag for future retrieval.

## Room F6: Common Room

More books here. These are a pile of mostly unredacted historical documents
which can be worth selling to an interested foreign collector. The dead
redactors don't have anything of value.

- History books: |$9000, 7.5kg|

## Room F4: Artifact Recovery

### XP Awards

- +1 for successfully deactivating the gate (claimed)

### Treasure

Dust of Emulation interacts with Used Magic Device and we don't use that here,
so let's replace it with a dose of Paut.

The PCs do find the secret panel, so they take everything in the list below.

- Ruined Cubic Gate: Lost its magic but still worth money as a

- Tome: +1 to Hidden Lore (Magic Items). |$500, 6kg|.

- Silver and mother-of-pearl ink pen: |$2000, 0.01kg|.

- Platinum disc depicting a leering angel: |$1000, 0.01kg|.

- 1 x Paut: Recovers 4 FP lost to magic. |$135, 0.5kg|.

- Headband of Strengthening Will +1 : Bronze circlet that grants +1 Will when
