An update to Rosalia, one of the player characters for my [solo Hell's Rebels
game][2]. Her description and backstory remains the same as her [starting
version][1], and we discuss what changed here.

I've played Rosalia in three different occasions so far. Once in another solo
game, once in a D&D 4 Zeitgeist play-by-post, and now. In the first one she was
a gunslinger and also psychic. In the second, she was pretty good with a
sword. I started out trying to make her a gunslinger again, but as mentioned in
a recent post I decided to rewrite her to be an archer because there's not going
to be a lot of advanced guns in this game.

Despite this, most of her contributions in combat have happened with her sword,
and with her Song of Humiliation. Outside of that she and Jania make an
excellent loot-selling team, and her interpersonal skills also make her an
awesome rebellion official.

There's a lot of cool utility in her spells that I have been lax in using,
perhaps that will change later. Her new fancy bow-harp and cornucopia should
make it easier for her to fulfill her new role, though I really should increase
her Bow skill and get her Fast-Draw (Arrow) as well.
