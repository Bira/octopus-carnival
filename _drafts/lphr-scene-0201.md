This post is part of [my solo Hell's Rebels campaign][1].

I'm going to use a new scene numbering format here to streamline my post titles
a bit: "aaxx", where the "aa" is the adventure number and the "xx" is the scene
number. This post is Adventure 02, Scene 01, so we can label it as "0201". There
are only six adventures and I'm highly unlikely to have more than 99 scenes for
any one of them.

# Intro

I think that by now, we can consider adventure 01 ("In Hell's Bright Shadow",
what a goofy name) to be complete. Our PCs officially reformed the Silver
Ravens, started growing the movement, and struck a few early blows against
Barzilai Thrune, capping those with a raid against their occupying force at the
old Sacred Archivist hideout in which they killed Nox, Thrune's personal
bodyguard.

In this post, we'll play through a couple of strategic turns to see if we can
level our rebellion up to its current maximum level of 5. Even if we don't get
there, we will still want to expand to a bigger hideout during Adventure 02 in
order to raise our level cap. Fortunately for us the adventure itself is exactly
about that.

We will not be using Mythic rules for this scene, as the strategic rules for
this campaign already include mechanisms for unforeseen events.
