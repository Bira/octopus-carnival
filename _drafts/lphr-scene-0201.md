This post is part of [my solo Hell's Rebels campaign][1].

I'm going to use a new scene numbering format here to streamline my post titles
a bit: "aaxx", where the "aa" is the adventure number and the "xx" is the scene
number. This post is Adventure 02, Scene 01, so we can label it as "0201". There
are only six adventures and I'm highly unlikely to have more than 99 scenes for
any one of them.

# Intro

I think that by now, we can consider adventure 01 ("In Hell's Bright Shadow",
what a goofy name) to be complete. Our PCs officially reformed the Silver
Ravens, started growing the movement, and struck a few early blows against
Barzilai Thrune, capping those with a raid against their occupying force at the
old Sacred Archivist hideout in which they killed Nox, Thrune's personal
bodyguard.

In this post, we'll play through a couple of strategic turns to see if we can
level our rebellion up to its current maximum level of 5. Even if we don't get
there, we will still want to expand to a bigger hideout during Adventure 02 in
order to raise our level cap. Fortunately for us the adventure itself is exactly
about that.

We will not be using Mythic rules for this scene, as the strategic rules for
this campaign already include mechanisms for unforeseen events.

# Turn 2

It's been a while since I've done this, so I'm having to reference both the
Player's Guide and my own conversion notes. Let's get to it.

## Turn 1 - Upkeep Phase

We pass our unomidified Loyalty check and lose only 5 supporters. We're still
rank 4. No deposits of withdrawals, no exceptional events or rank changes.

## Turn 1 - Activity Phase

We have 2 actions from our Rank and 1 from having a Strategist.

To refresh our memories: the organization itself can do Change Officer Role,
Dismiss Team, Guarantee Event, Lie Low, Recruit Supporters, Recruit Team and
Upgrade Team, as well as Special actions to resolve events.

In addition to those, our teams grant us Earn Money, Gather Information, Reduce
Danger, and Secure Cache.

We don't really have any pressing story-related actions that we need to take,
and we're already at our current team maximum, so let's focus on growing our
rebellion and upgrading our teams until we reach Rank 5.

Therefore, our first action is to Recruit Supporters. We succeed and recruit 13
supporters, bringing our total to 36 and getting us over the threshold for
Rank 5. Yay! This will only take effect on the next turn, though.

Our next action is Upgrade Team, and we'll upgrade our Sneaks to Thieves. This
costs $1250 and requires no test.

For our Bonus Action, we'll use the Crow Sisters to Earn Gold, and our success
here gives us a piddly $80. Hm, not worth it.

## Turn 1 - Event Phase

Kintargo's Danger rating is 20, our Notoriety is 5, we have a 25% chance of an
event. Do we need to double it? I'm saying no, as this is a new adventure and
this is our first event roll for it.

No event this week.

# Turn 2

Let's do a second turn so we can actually reach our maximum rank and do a little
bit more to get our rebellion ready for Adventure 2.

## Turn 2 - Upkeep Phase

We pass our Loyalty roll and lose 2 supporters to attrition.

Our Notoriety and Treasury are fine.

We rank up to 5! The rebellion's team limit increases by 1, and our PCs gain a
XP award. This means they get a flat 15 character points each!

The PCs see they have $21535 in their communal treasury, so they decide to
deposit $10K of that into the rebellion's treasury. That's 25 gold coins, which
we'll need to recruit our new team and to keep upgrading our existing ones.

## Turn 2 - Activity Phase

We still have 2 standard actions and 1 bonus action. Our available actions are
the same, plus Activate Safehouse from our newly upgraded Thieves.

Our first action for the week is to Upgrade Team again, taking our Thieves all
the way to Spies. This costs a whopping $5000 and I'm sure the Pathfinder
version didn't expect us to have this much money at this time. But I can do it
now, so I will. Spies are invaluable and I think we might want Saboteurs too in
the future.

Our second action is another Upgrade Team, taking our Freedom Fighters to
Infiltrator status for $1250.

As a result of these, we gain the Covert Action and Rescue Character actions.

We don't have any story pressures now but we do have a doubled event chance, so
let's Reduce Danger as our bonus action using the Infiltrators. Rexus is the
only one who can help with this since it's a bonus action, but we still succeed
by enough to temporarily reduce our Danger by 11, to 9.

I had to write up the Reduce Danger action, since it was missing from the big
rebellion rules post:

- Reduce Danger (Freedom Fighters, Infiltrators, Cabalists, Spellcasters): Roll
  Security -2 and reduce the city's Danger for this week and the next by 5 +
  Margin of Success. Failure instead increases Notoriety by 1d4 and Danger by 5
  for the same amount of time.

It has also been added to that post.

## Turn 2 - Event Phase

Okay, we didn't get an event last turn, so now the chance doubles. Base chance
is 9+5 = 14% because we reduced the city's Danger, doubled to 28%. We get a 2,
so something happens despite our precautions.

We roll 1d100+9 (Danger) on the event table and come up with 102. Criminy,
that's a failed protest! Looks like our supporters try to set up a big protest
against Thrune but it flops, so we lose 2d6 supporters unless we pass a Security
-6 test.

Only Jade can apply her skills to this test, since it's an event resolution, but
she can use skills from the Partisan category and she has all three of those at
good levels, so let's see... three normal successes give us +3, so our effective
skill is 11. We fail by 2, and lose 10 supporters. Whoops.

This brings us back down to 23. We don't lose ranks, but we now have a longer
way to go until we rank up again at 40+ supporters. This will only be possible
at the end of Adventure 02, but I guess we should still keep up our recruiting
efforts to build up a buffer. We might even be able to rank up multiple times
when we unlock our progression.

# Ending Rebellion Sheet

I think this is enough. The flopped protest seems like a good stopping point,
and it will be hanging over our heroes as the "tactical" part of the adventure
starts.
