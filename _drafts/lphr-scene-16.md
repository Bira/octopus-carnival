# Intro

We're officially done with Adventure 01. Its capstone dungeon is cleared and
looted, and Barzilai has lost a large number of his loyal followers, including
an important lieutenant. He also definitely knows _someone_ out there is
engaging in organized resistance against him.

But for now, our heroes can sit back and spend the hard-earned points they got
at the end of the last adventure. Everyone got 14 points, and some of them had
been saving up even before. Everyone might be able to afford some big-ticket
advancements!

And not just that, between adventures is a very good time for our player (me) to
stop and reassess our character builds, and "refactor" them if they aren't
working well or aren't fun. And it's also a very good time for our GM (also me)
to maybe introduce some house rules that would have been potentially disruptive
to implement mid-adventure.

# [GM] New House Rule Time!

There's a few rules I'd really like to try here. I've made posts about them all
before, so I'm going to link them here.

- [Merging Weapon Skills][1]
- [Merging Shield Skills][2]
- [Merging Unarmed Combat Skills][3]

These is going to change our PC's character sheets, and I even already planned
out how this would happen [recently][4], so what's left to do here is apply
those changes. Any points saved by the changes go into the unspent pile.

# [Player] Advancing our heroes

We'll go over them one by one in alphabetical order, and detailing both the
refactorings and the advancement for each of them.

## Arcturus Pankrator, Knight of the Deep

Our boy Art ends up with the following new combat skills from the new house rule:

- Grappling (A) DX+5 {20} - 20
- Knife (A) DX+1 {4} - 16
- Unarmed Striking (A) DX+2 {8} - 17

This frees up 4 points for him, meaning he has a total of 18 to spend. That's
not enough for Extra Attack or Unarmed Master, which are the big ticket items
I'm after. He just got a pretty cool suit of armor so I'm betting that he won't
need any more DR from Tough Skin or other powers, either.

So I think I'm just going to hold until I do get those 25 points, and then I'm
getting Extra Attack 1.

# Jade Irinka, The Sixth Raven

Jade's Saber skill changes its name to Fencing and becomes a little more
conceptually broad, but otherwise remains the same, as does her number of
unspent points. However, she has 20 of those!

There's a lot of things I could do with this, but I think I'm going to
be straightforward and get her 2 points of ST, bringing it up to 13. That lets
her do a lot more damage with her backsword swings, potentially wear heavier
armor, and gives her a tiny bit more HP.

# Jania Moonshadow, the Phantom Thief

Jania has 17 points, and I think we're way overdue for that third level of
Spell-Thief Expertise for her. That's 11 points, and it will make most of her
spells hit that magic skill level of 15. The one exception is Great Haste, which
remains at 14, but we can remedy that by spending 1 extra point.

4 of the remaining 5 points to go buying Observation at 15, and the last one
goes to increasing Merchant to 14.

# Rosalia Savory, the Mysterious Satirist

Rosalia's Saber skill changes its name to Fencing, and before I spend her 14
free points I'd like to refactor her a little.

Right now I'm setting her up to be good with guns because that's what I had done
in a previous solo game, but I don't think I want to do that here after
all. In the last delve she could only fire that gun once, and she missed. While
there are several solutions to the rate-of-fire problem, I think I'm going to
reserve that for Jade, and go in a different direction with Rosalia. I'm going
to make her an archer instead.

So if we remove her Off-Hand Weapon Training perk and her Gunslinger (One-Handed
Guns) advantage, we free up 16 points. Her Guns (Pistol) skill at 16 becomes Bow
at 15. We're left with 30 free points. 20 of those go towards Heroic Archer, 4
go to bringing Bow back to 16. 3 of the remaining 6 points go to increasing
Singing to 18. We'll keep the other 3 in reserve for now.

# Urist Stortebeker, The Last Archivist

The skill changes don't affect Urist's skills in any way that needs renaming or
rewriting. I'll spend 4 points to increase his Axe/Mace to 15, and another 4 to
increase Shield. The remaining 6 go to buy 2 points of Energy Reserve.
