I've been stressing out about real-life fascists these past few days, so I
thought I'd destress a bit by publishing another post about people fighting
fantasy fascists in my [solo Hell's Rebels campaign][2]

This is an update to Jania. Her description and backstory remain the same and
can be see in the post for her [starting version][1].

After playing through Adventure 1 I can say Jania has been working pretty much
as expected. A thiefy wizard, and a wizard thief. She has handled all the
trap-finding and lock-opening that she needed to, and her spells have been
occasionally useful as well. The standout here were the repeated uses of Glass
Wall to help with the breaching-and-clearing of Hocum's Phantasmagorium at the
end of the adventure.

I didn't expect a lot out of her in combat since she's a Thief-type, but she was
surprisingly useful when she got to hit people in the skull with her cane while
Art held them down. She's switching to a smallsword now, so I wonder how that
will change things. Her ranged combat on the other hand has been as
disappointing as expected, since it takes a while to charge up an Ice Dagger and
it often misses or gets dodged.

Her advancement has been focusing more on utility spells and skills,
particularly the ones needed to help run the rebellion. Like the rest of her
companions, she started out with 250 points and currently sits at 289 with 0
unspent points. The biggest expenditure here was a much-needed improvement of
Spell-Thief Expertise from 2 to 3.
