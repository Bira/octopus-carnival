An update to Urist Stortebeker, one of the player characters for my [solo Hell's
Rebels campaign][2]. His description and backstory remain the same as his
[starting version][1], and we discuss what changed here.

I tried leading Urist in a "fightier" direction, as befits an axe-wielding
dwarf, but my success was mixed here. He's generally hard to hurt and his new
armor should make him even more so, but he hasn't been the most successful at
hurting enemies. That said, his healing spells have been literal life-savers
here, and his buff and utility magic has pulled its weight.

He started at 250 points and is now at 294, with zero unspent points. This is a
higher total than the others because I noticed I had too many disadvantages on
his starting sheet and removed one. I don't believe it will cause any imbalance.

That said I did try to make him fightier. One level each in Axe/Mace, Shield,
ST, and Higher Purpose (Slay Demons) make him hit better and harder, doubly so
against demons. He can also wear heavier armor.

He also improved or acquired several skills that help run the rebellion's
strategic layer. Magic-wise, we have a couple of levels of Energy Reserve to
help with the casting, which is slightly better for that purpose than more
Fatigue because it recovers in parallel.
