An update to one of the player characters for my [solo Hell's Rebels
campaign][2]. This is Art after the end of Adventure 01, with all advancements
in stats and gear included.

His description and backstory remain the same and can be seen in the post for
his [starting version][1].

Art is our second-best combatant in terms of sheer effectiveness, a small
distance behind Jade. She can just stab fools, while he usually has to get in
close and wrestle with them for a bit before doing damage. While doing this he's
vulnerable to stuff like damaging auras, poisonous or caustic skin, spiked armor
and other similar effects. And, of course, to other foes deciding to strike at
him mid-grapple.

But he's also tough enough to take the hits, at least for a while, and he's been
extremely _fun_ to play. When I'm stuck thinking about optimal tactics or
in-character motivations for choosing this plan over the other, I can always
count on the little Arcturus in my head to go "forget all this! CHAAAARGE!". And
even if he takes a couple more turns to dispatch an opponent, that opponent is
doing _nothing else_ but grappling with him during all that time.

His backstory as the Knight of the Deep hasn't mattered much yet, but him being
an Atlantean and thus unhindered by water has come in handy in a couple of spots
already.

Arcturus started out with the default 250 points for DFRPG PCs, and at the end
of Adventure 01 he's currently at 271 points plus 18 unspent ones for a total
of 289. He's saving up for one of Extra Attack or Unarmed Master and should get
it early in adventure 02.

Points-wise, he's the character who benefitted the most from our combat skill
house rules, and he also boasts increased Lifting ST to make him an even more
terrifying wrestler.

Gear-wise, he now has a very nice suit of magic armor that was looted from a
boss and properly purified, and an amulet that makes him very resistant to
magic, so some of his biggest weaknesses are now nicely shored up.
