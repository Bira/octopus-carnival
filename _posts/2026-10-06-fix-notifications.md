It has come to my attention that I've been less than fastidious about proper
linking and formatting of my posts. Or, in other words, I've been mostly moving
them from my "drafts" folder to the "public posts" one and then forgetting all
about them without checking for bugs.

This post here is a notice that a bunch of these bugs have been fixed.

Every post in both my [Let's Read][1] and [Let's Play][2] of Hell's Rebels
that contained a broken link to their respective project page now has the
correct link.

The very long loot ledger from [Adventure 01, Scene 15][3] contained some
characters that Markdown interpreted as a table, and as a result looked all
wonky. These characters have been removed.

The post for [Adventure 01, Scene 16][4] wasn't showing up at all because its
filename was incorrect. This has been fixed.
